Process lab

Follow one process from a lexflow model: the step you are on, each deadline counted from the law's own words and the state's holiday statute, and what the law says happens if it passes. Setup is meant to link here, but does not yet.

Your cases stay in this browser. They are kept in this browser's storage and nowhere else. What the page sends to this lexflow: the state and process you choose; for the state of a case you start or open, one request for the list of its governments and their office channels (made whether or not the case has a place), so the place search and "where to send" run here and your place is never sent; and, to count deadlines, the case's event types, dates and calendar choices (never your notes, label or place), which lexflow answers and does not keep. The web server's access log records each request's path and time and your network address. An outcome is shared only if you click Share anonymised outcome and send what is shown. Browsers can clear stored data (Safari after 7 days without a visit), so export a case you need to keep. Information, not legal advice.
